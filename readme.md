# How Parliaments Polarize: Legislative Replacement and Adaptation in Chile

**Jorge Fábrega**  
CICS - Universidad del Desarrollo

This repository accompanies the paper **"How Parliaments Polarize: Legislative Replacement and Adaptation in Chile"**.

The paper studies the evolution of ideological polarization in the Chilean Chamber of Deputies from 2002-06 to 2022-26 using roll-call votes and dynamically anchored ideal-point estimates.

## Main finding

Ideological polarization in the Chilean Chamber of Deputies increased by approximately **76%** between the 2002-06 and 2022-26 legislative periods.

![Dynamic ideological polarization in the Chilean Chamber of Deputies, full sample](D:/varios/monitor_congreso/pape/polarizacion/docs/PA version 2/polarization_ssrn/figures/chile_polarization_full_sample.png)


## What the paper shows

- Polarization increased steadily over six legislative periods.
- Successive cohorts of entrants appear at progressively higher levels of ideological extremity.
- A replacement-only counterfactual reproduces essentially the entire observed increase in polarization.
- Within-legislator change after entry shows no systematic trend toward greater extremity.

The results suggest that the long-run rise in parliamentary polarization in Chile is explained primarily by **legislative replacement** rather than by systematic **within-legislator adaptation**.
